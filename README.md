# SkyWest Airlines

SkyWest Airlines is the largest regional airline in North America, headquartered in St. George, Utah. Operating under partnership agreements with United Airlines, Delta Air Lines, American Airlines, and Alaska Airlines, SkyWest serves over 256 destinations across the United States, Canada, and Mexico with a fleet of 500+ Bombardier regional jets (CRJ200, CRJ550, CRJ900). Founded in 1972, SkyWest carries over 46 million passengers annually operating as United Express, Delta Connection, American Eagle, and Alaska SkyWest.
